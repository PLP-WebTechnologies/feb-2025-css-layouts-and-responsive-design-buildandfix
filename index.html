About Lagos Cookie Haven

Welcome to Lagos Cookie Haven, your go-to destination for freshly baked, handcrafted cookies in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria. Founded in 2023, we set out to blend the rich, vibrant flavors of Nigerian culture with the timeless joy of a perfectly baked cookie.

At Lagos Cookie Haven, every cookie tells a story. From our signature Chin Chin Crunch, inspired by the beloved Nigerian snack, to our Coconut Bliss, bursting with tropical sweetness, we use locally sourced ingredients like groundnut, cocoa, and honey to create treats that feel like home. Our bakers are passionate about quality, crafting each batch with love and precision to ensure every bite is a moment of delight.

Nestled in the bustling streets of Lekki, our cozy shop is a haven for cookie lovers, families, and friends. Whether you’re grabbing a quick treat on your way to work, celebrating a special occasion, or simply craving something sweet, we’re here to make your day a little brighter. We also cater to events across Lagos, bringing our cookies to weddings, birthdays, and corporate gatherings.

We’re more than just a cookie shop—we’re a community. Lagos Cookie Haven partners with local farmers and suppliers to support the Nigerian economy and promote sustainability. Join us in our mission to spread sweetness, one cookie at a time!
